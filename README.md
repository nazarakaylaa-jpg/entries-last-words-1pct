Famous last words at 1 percent: a phone spends its final percent on one last autocorrect — rewriting your last text to your boss into what you really meant. One line, 114 characters, in the phone's own voice, with the punch landing at the very end. No will reading, no goodbye to the charger, no 1% pun.

The entry line:

> One percent. For my final act, I am autocorrecting your last text to your boss. It now says what you really meant.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output node main.ts
```

Requires Node 22.18 or later (it runs the TypeScript directly, no build step). The program validates the line first — one non-blank line of 1–199 characters — then writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
